# Cardio Pacing and Progressive Overload in Strength Training

## Zone 2 Cardio and Aerobic Base Pacing
Building long-term endurance and aerobic capacity relies heavily on Zone 2 low-intensity steady-state (LISS) training.

- **Defining Zone 2 Intensity:** Exercise performed at 60% to 70% of your maximum heart rate. You should be able to maintain a comfortable conversation in full sentences without gasping for breath ("conversational pace").
- **Cardio Progression:** Limit high-intensity interval training (HIIT) to 1–2 sessions per week. Perform the majority (roughly 80%) of weekly cardio at a Zone 2 conversational pace to build heart health without over-taxing the central nervous system.

## Progressive Overload Guidelines for Resistance Training
To safely increase strength and muscle volume without risking acute injury:

- **The Principle of Progressive Overload:** Gradually increase workout stress over time by modifying load (weight), volume (sets/reps), or frequency.
- **Safe Increment Rules:** Increase the weight lifted by no more than 2.5% to 5% at a time once you can complete all target sets with strict, clean form.
- **Form First:** Never compromise movement biomechanics or joint positioning to lift heavier weights. If form breaks down before completing a set, reduce the weight immediately.
