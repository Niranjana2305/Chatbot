# Pre-Workout, Intra-Workout, and Post-Workout Fueling

## Pre-Workout Nutrition Timing
Providing your body with accessible glycogen stores improves workout stamina and prevents premature fatigue.

- **1 to 3 Hours Before Exercise:** Eat a balanced meal containing complex carbohydrates and moderate lean protein (e.g., oatmeal with berries and protein powder, or rice with chicken).
- **30 to 60 Minutes Before Exercise:** Consume a light, easily digestible simple carbohydrate snack if needed (e.g., a banana, toast, or rice cake) while minimizing heavy fats and fiber that slow digestion.

## Hydration and Intra-Workout Fueling
- **Short Workouts (< 60 minutes):** Plain water is sufficient for hydration during moderate workouts under an hour.
- **Longer or High-Heat Workouts (> 60 minutes):** Consume fluids with added sodium, potassium, and magnesium (electrolytes) alongside 30–60 grams of simple carbohydrates per hour to maintain blood glucose and fluid balance.

## Post-Workout Recovery Window
After completing intense workouts or strength sessions, prioritize muscle protein synthesis and glycogen replenishment.

- **Recovery Intake:** Consume 20 to 40 grams of high-quality protein along with carbohydrates within 1 to 2 hours post-workout.
- **Rehydration:** Drink roughly 16–24 ounces of water or electrolyte beverage for every pound of body weight lost through sweat during the exercise session.
