# Sleep Hygiene, Hydration, Heat Safety, and Nutritional Health

## 1. Sleep Hygiene and Circadian Health
### Symptoms of Sleep Deprivation
- Cognitive impairment, impaired motor coordination, reduced reaction time, mood instability, higher systemic inflammation, and impaired muscle tissue synthesis.
### Causes
- Irregular sleep/wake schedule, late-night blue light exposure, caffeine intake within 6–8 hours of bedtime, or high ambient bedroom temperature.
### Optimal Sleep Habit Guidelines
- Maintain consistent wake and sleep times daily (even on weekends).
- Keep sleep environment dark, quiet, and cool (~65°F / 18°C).
- Discontinue screen exposure 30–60 minutes prior to sleep.

## 2. Hydration and Heat Stress
### Dehydration
- **Symptoms**: Thirst, dark yellow/amber urine, dry mouth, headache, fatigue, dizziness, and reduced athletic performance.
- **Causes**: Inadequate fluid intake relative to sweat losses, high humidity, or prolonged exercise.
- **Prevention**: Consume 2–3 liters of water daily as a baseline; add 16–24 oz of fluid per hour of vigorous exercise.

### Heat Exhaustion vs. Heat Stroke
- **Heat Exhaustion Symptoms**: Heavy sweating, clammy/pale skin, rapid weak pulse, muscle cramps, dizziness, nausea, headache.
  - *Action*: Move to shade/AC, sip cold water, apply cool damp cloths.
- **Heat Stroke Symptoms (MEDICAL EMERGENCY)**: Body temperature above 103°F (39.4°C), hot red dry or damp skin, rapid strong pulse, confusion, slurred speech, loss of consciousness.
  - *Action*: Call 911 immediately. Rapidly cool person with ice baths/packs.

## 3. Safe Nutrition Practices
### Symptoms of Under-fueling / Excessive Caloric Restriction
- Persistent dizziness, chronic lethargy, hair loss, loss of menstrual cycle (amenorrhea in females), extreme food obsession, or sudden severe mood drops.
### Causes
- Aggressive calorie cutting, skipping essential macronutrient groups, or severe fasting protocols coupled with exercise habits.
### Guidance
- Avoid extreme caloric restrictions. Habits should focus on sustainable, nutrient-dense meal consistency rather than severe starvation.
