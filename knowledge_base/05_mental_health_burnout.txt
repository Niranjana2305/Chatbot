# Mental Health, Burnout, and Habit Psychology

## 1. Burnout and Toxic Habit Perfectionism
### Symptoms of Habit Burnout
- Feeling overwhelmed by streak counters, obsessive anxiety over missing a day, guilt surrounding uncompleted habits, emotional numbness, and loss of enjoyment in self-improvement.
### Causes
- Setting unrealistic initial expectations ("all-or-nothing" mentality), relying strictly on willpower without adequate rest, or equating personal self-worth to habit streak counts.
### Management Strategies
- **The 2-Minute Rule**: Scale the habit down to its smallest non-intimidating version (e.g., instead of "Read 30 minutes", change habit to "Read 1 page").
- **Never Miss Twice Rule**: Accept that life disruptions happen. Frame missing a single day as normal recovery, not a total failure.

## 2. Differentiating Stress from Clinical Mental Health Concerns
### General Stress / Habit Resistance
- Temporary reluctance to perform daily routine, mild procrastination, or temporary tiredness. Resolves after starting the habit or taking a planned rest day.

### Clinical Anxiety or Depression Red Flags
- Persistent feeling of hopelessness or worthlessness lasting longer than 2 weeks, inability to perform daily self-care tasks, panic attacks, or thoughts of self-harm.
- **Action**: The habit bot must acknowledge its limitations, validate the user's emotional state, and encourage contacting a qualified mental health professional or crisis helpline (e.g., dialing 988 in the US/Canada).
